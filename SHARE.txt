TradePilot Full Package
=======================

Build on this PC:
  Make-FullPackage.bat
  Make-FullPackage.bat with-env   ← includes your .env for your other computers

Output:
  share\TradePilot-Full-Portable.zip

On the other PC: unzip → read START-HERE.txt → TradePilot.bat

Needs MetaTrader 5 on that PC. Python is bundled in the full package.
